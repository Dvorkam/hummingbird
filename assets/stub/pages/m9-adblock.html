Ad Block Lite

Added in M9 (stories 9.4.1 / 9.4.2). Requests are matched against declarative rules natively, before anything is sent. No extension JavaScript runs on the request path.

An ad blocker cannot be shown with pixels. Blocking is the absence of something, so this page tells you what should be missing and the log tells you whether it was. Read this page next to the log.

First-party content (must always render)

This paragraph comes from example.dev. It must look identical whether the blocker is on or off — a blocker that changes the page it is protecting has failed.

How to check it

The extension is enabled by default. To compare, disable it in assets/config/browser.ini:

[extensions]
ad-block-lite = disabled
What to look forBlocker ONBlocker OFF
[filter] blocked in the log3 or moreabsent
Nodes under “appended by the third-party script”none25
Third-party panel backgroundwhiteyellow
Missing-API warnings in the logfewermore
First-party paragraph aboveidentical in both

The last row is the one that matters most: the measurable win is real only if the page it protects is unharmed.
