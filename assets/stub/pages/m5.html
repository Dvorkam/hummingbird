Tabs (manual check)

Press Ctrl+T to open a new tab, Ctrl+Left/Right to switch, Ctrl+W to close. Typing or scrolling in one tab must not affect the others.

Extension dark mode

The built-in dark-mode extension injects CSS into every page. With it enabled, both cards render dark (disable via HB_EXTENSIONS_DISABLE=dark-mode or assets/config/browser.ini).
